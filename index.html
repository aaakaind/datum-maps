DATUM

A free, open map of the world. Search places, get walking, cycling and driving directions with turn-by-turn navigation, and keep any part of the world on your device to use with no connection. No account, no ads.

Where your requests go

  • Map tiles load from OpenFreeMap. Like any tile server, it sees your IP address and the area you are viewing.
  • Searches go to Photon, komoot's public geocoder, when you type 3 or more characters. Tapping the map sends that point to Photon to look up the nearest address.
  • Routes go to Valhalla on the FOSSGIS public server when you ask for directions, and so do the start points of Reachable from here areas.
  • Terrain relief, when you switch it on, loads elevation tiles from the Terrain Tiles dataset on AWS Open Data.
  • Locate me asks your browser for your position. It stays on your device; it is only sent when you use it as a route start, like any other point.
  • Saved places and recent searches are kept in this browser's local storage and never leave your device.
  • The address bar holds the view, place or route you are looking at, so a link you copy or share opens the same thing. That link is only sent where you send it.
  • Inside a downloaded region pack, search and routing run on your device and nothing is sent.
  • Category lists (Coffee, Parks…) are read from the map tiles already on your screen. No request is made.
  • DATUM itself adds no accounts, cookies or analytics.

Offline areas

The Offline tab lists every country and territory, every state or province and more than 7,000 cities, and can also download whatever is on screen. An area is stored at one of three levels. Streets (cities, up to about 2,500 map tiles) keeps every road and path, so the map, search, and walking, cycling and driving directions all work with no connection. Roads (regions and smaller countries) keeps the map to town level and every through road, for driving directions. Map (the largest countries and the whole world) keeps a lower-zoom map only.

The road network is built on your device from the same OpenStreetMap vector tiles the map draws, and stored in the browser. Ottawa–Gatineau is also available as a region pack with richer place details from the Overture Maps Foundation (release 2026-09-23.1).

Emergency (SOS)

The SOS tab shows your coordinates in large type, the emergency number for the country you are in, and the nearest hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, police and fire stations, shelters and fuel, with one tap for directions. Inside a downloaded area all of it works with no connection. Share position sends your coordinates through your phone's own share sheet or a text message; DATUM sends them nowhere itself. Place data comes from OpenStreetMap and can be incomplete: call the emergency number first.

Navigation

Start follows your position along the route with spoken turn instructions and finds a new route if you leave it. Preview drive plays the route back without using your position. Keep your eyes on the road and obey signs: the map can be wrong or out of date.

World view

Zoomed out, DATUM draws the world in the Equal Earth projection (Šavrič, Patterson and Jenny, 2018), which keeps every country's area true to scale. The UN General Assembly backed it on 4 September 2026 as a more accurate picture of the world. Land, lakes and borders come from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain. Borders are shown as they stand on the ground; disputed or unsettled lines are dashed. Names and lines do not imply endorsement by AKA IND.

How times are estimated, and traffic

On-device routes search road by road with turn and junction delays: walking 4.9 km/h, cycling 15 to 19 km/h by surface, driving at a speed for the road type and whether it runs through a built-up area. In checks against Valhalla on the same trips the times agree to within about 10%. Turn restrictions are not in the map tiles and are not applied.

There is no free live traffic feed for the whole world. By default driving times use a typical-traffic model: a rush-hour curve by time of day and day of week, applied by road type. It is an estimate, not a measurement. Add your own TomTom key to use live traffic instead. Valhalla routes from the public server carry no traffic.
