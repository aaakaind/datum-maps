DATUM

A free, open map of the world. Search places, get walking, cycling and driving directions, and download region packs that search and route on your device. No account, no ads.

Where your requests go

  • Map tiles load from OpenFreeMap. Like any tile server, it sees your IP address and the area you are viewing.
  • Searches go to Photon, komoot's public geocoder, when you type 3 or more characters. Tapping the map sends that point to Photon to look up the nearest address.
  • Routes go to Valhalla on the FOSSGIS public server when you ask for directions.
  • Inside a downloaded region pack, search and routing run on your device and nothing is sent.
  • Category lists (Coffee, Parks…) are read from the map tiles already on your screen. No request is made.
  • DATUM itself adds no accounts, cookies or analytics.

Region packs

Ottawa–Gatineau is the first pack. It holds roads, paths and places from the Overture Maps Foundation (release 2026-09-23.1), about 3.9 MB, and is stored in your browser after the first download.

How times are estimated

Valhalla routes use the router's own speed model. Region-pack routes assume steady speeds: walking 4.9 km/h, cycling 15 km/h, driving at the posted limit or a default for the road type. Neither includes live traffic, so treat times as estimates.
